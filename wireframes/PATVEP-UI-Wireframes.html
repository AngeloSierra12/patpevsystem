PATVEP Hostel & Canteen

Layout studies for the four screens assigned to the Frontend and UI/UX developer. Deliberately rough: grey blocks, placeholder lines and region labels only. A wireframe settles where things sit and what outranks what. Colour, wording and real records are decided afterwards, on purpose.

Sheet 03

Audit Trail

WBS 7.1

One table, nothing competing with it. The filter bar sits above the rows rather than in a side panel, because narrowing the log is the only thing anyone does here. No action column: the trail is read, never edited — that is what makes it evidence.

Sheet 04

Reports

WBS 9.1

Output on the left, controls on the right. The controls are a short stacked form, not a toolbar, because choosing a report is a sequence — type, then range, then generate — and a stack reads in that order. Output keeps the width so a wide table does not have to scroll sideways.
